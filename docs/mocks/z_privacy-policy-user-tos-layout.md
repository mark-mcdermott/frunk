# Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Frunk Inc. (“Frunk”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

---

## 01 — Introduction

Frunk is built to store the things that matter—securely and privately. This policy outlines how we handle your data with care and respect.

---

## 02 — What We Collect

We collect only what’s necessary to provide the service:

- Account information
- Documents and files you choose to store
- Basic usage data for reliability and performance

---

> 🔒 **We never sell your data. Ever.**

---

## 03 — How We Use Data

We use your data to:

- Keep your documents organized and accessible
- Sync across devices
- Improve reliability and performance

---

## 04 — Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your data except when required by law or to operate essential infrastructure.

---

## 05 — Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard encryption and secure storage practices.

---

> 💡 **Your data belongs to you. You can export or delete it at any time.**

---

## 06 — Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

---

## 07 — Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your data
- Request deletion
- Export your information

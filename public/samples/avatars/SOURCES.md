# Sample avatars

Frunk's own renders — `violet.webp`, `silver.webp` and `light.webp` — exported at 512×512
from the PNGs in `frunk-proj/branding/images/` (outside the repo). They are the profile
pictures of the seeded accounts and of every demo cloned from them
(`scripts/seed-office.ts`). No third-party material, so nothing to attribute.
